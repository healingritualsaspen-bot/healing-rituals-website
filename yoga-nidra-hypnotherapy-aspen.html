Common questions

The Guided Experience

What is the session called?

Lauren’s unique process is called Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming. Yoga Nidra and hypnotherapy are useful search terms for understanding some of the guided-rest and intentional-language principles that inform the work.

Will I be asleep?

Awareness can move between wakefulness, dreamlike imagery and very deep rest. Many clients remain aware of the guidance while the body feels profoundly relaxed.

Is the journey personalized?

Completely. Two or three intentions are collected beforehand and woven into the language, metaphors, imagery, affirmations and recording.

Can I reuse the experience?

Yes. Your personalized recording can be revisited for falling asleep, daytime rest or continued reinforcement of your current intentions.
