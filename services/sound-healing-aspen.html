The approach

An experience you can receive

A sound healing session is a listening experience, not a performance. You rest while an intentional soundscape unfolds around you. There is nothing to learn in advance and no correct emotional response. The invitation is to notice what you hear and feel without having to turn it into an explanation.

At Healing Rituals, sound can be an experience of its own or part of Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming, Reiki or gentle breathwork. The instruments, pacing and amount of spoken guidance are chosen around your intentions and sensory preferences. Quiet moments are as important as the tones themselves.

For someone who spends the day making decisions or caring for other people, a session can offer time without requests or responsibilities. Others are drawn to the emotional quality of music, the physical sensation of resonance or a spiritually meaningful way to slow down. These are different reasons to choose the same practice.

The phrase vibrational medicine describes the role sound and felt vibration play in Lauren’s work. The research articles below focus on measurable outcomes such as tension, mood and relaxation, and explain how those studies relate to a private sound experience.

Planning your time

A session that fits your life

An Aspen sound session can fit into a quiet afternoon during a Snowmass stay or a longer private wellness experience. Local residents and visitors can ask about individual, couples and small-group formats before choosing a date.

A common question

Can the sound be made softer?

Yes. Share sound sensitivities, hearing concerns and preferences beforehand. You can ask for quieter playing, more distance from an instrument or a pause at any time.
