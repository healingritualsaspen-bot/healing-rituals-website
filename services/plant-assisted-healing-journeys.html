Meet your guide

The approach

Start with the questions, not an itinerary

Interest in plant medicine often begins with something personal: grief, a life transition, a recurring pattern or a wish to reconnect with purpose. Preparation is time to explore that interest without pressure to decide quickly or expect a particular revelation.

At Healing Rituals, plant-assisted therapy is held as a highly personal, carefully supported process. Preparation, setting, guided experience, subconscious work, sound, energy and integration are woven together so the journey feels coherent from beginning to end.

An inquiry with Healing Rituals begins with your intentions and the change you are ready to welcome. Guided rest, sound, gentle breath, Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming, energy work and reflective integration are then shaped into a deeply personal path forward.

Integration gives an experience somewhere to go. Rather than treating every image as an instruction, it invites reflection: What felt meaningful? What remains unclear? Which small action would help you live more closely to the values you noticed? A renewed boundary or a more honest conversation may matter more than a dramatic life change.

Every journey begins here

Your 60–90 Minute Introductory Experience

Every client begins with a private introductory session lasting 60–90 minutes. This is not simply a consultation. It is an immersive first experience designed to help you feel the work, become comfortable in the space and begin revealing the intentions that will shape your deeper journey.

You will be supported through restorative sound, gentle breath, a Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming journey and a small portion of passive energy clearing. Each element is received while resting comfortably. There is nothing to perform, prove or force.

This first session allows us to become clear on what you are ready to heal, release, understand or create. It also gives you the opportunity to see and feel the deeply beautiful, divine and safe setting in which your future journey will unfold.

Questions & answers

Before Your Plant-Assisted Healing Journey

How does working together begin?

Every client begins with a private 60–90 minute introductory experience. You will receive restorative sound, gentle breath, a Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming journey and a small portion of passive energy clearing. This helps us clarify your intentions while allowing you to experience the setting, Lauren’s guidance and the feeling of the work before moving into a deeper journey.

Is the introductory session only a consultation?

No. It is an actual guided healing experience. Conversation and intention setting are part of the session, but you will also rest comfortably and receive an immersive combination of sound, subconscious journeying, gentle breath and energy clearing.

Do I need to know my intentions before the first session?

No. You may arrive with clear intentions, a general feeling that something needs to shift or simply curiosity about what is possible. The introductory experience helps bring the most meaningful themes into focus without pressure to arrive with perfect words.

What makes the journey personal to me?

Your intentions, emotional patterns, language, desired feelings and vision for your life become the foundation of the experience. The guidance, metaphors, sound, energetic layers and integration are thoughtfully shaped around you rather than delivered from a generic script.

Will I need to talk, respond or perform during the journey?

No. The experience is designed to be beautifully passive. You rest, listen and receive. Sound, gentle breath, subconscious guidance and energy work are layered around you without requiring constant conversation, concentration or effort.

How are sound and energy work used?

Restorative sound and vibration help soften mental noise and deepen the inner experience. Passive energy clearing may draw from Reiki, polarity and craniosacral-informed work. These elements are woven gently into the journey so they feel like one continuous healing environment.

What is Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming?

It is Lauren’s signature passive guided process, using personalized imagery, metaphor, intentional language, sound and spacious awareness to move beyond habitual thinking and into the deeper inner landscape where feelings, memories, patterns and new possibilities can be experienced differently.

What happens after the introductory experience?

Together, we clarify what surfaced, what you are ready to explore and what kind of deeper journey would be most supportive. Your next experience is then curated around your intentions, comfort, timing and desired transformation.

How does integration become part of the work?

Integration helps the insight and emotional movement of a journey become part of daily life. It may include reflective conversation, subconscious reinforcement, personalized practices, your reusable recording and clear ways to carry new feelings, choices and patterns forward.

Can I travel to Aspen for this work?

Yes. Private inquiries are welcomed from across Colorado, throughout the United States and internationally. Sessions can be thoughtfully planned around time in Aspen, Snowmass, Basalt, Carbondale and the wider Roaring Fork Valley.

Can couples experience this work?

Yes. Couples may receive individual preparation and personal inner work alongside shared experiences and integration. The process can support deeper listening, compassion, emotional communication, trust and a more conscious way of meeting one another.
