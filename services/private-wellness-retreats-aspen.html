The approach

An itinerary that leaves something out

When life is crowded, the instinct is often to make a retreat productive too: more sessions, more practices, more goals. A private experience offers the chance to choose carefully. What would actually support you now—rest, reflection, connection or space to hear yourself think?

Healing Rituals creates experiences for individuals, couples and small groups, drawing on sound, Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming, energy work and gentle breath. A focused session or a series of sessions can be discussed around your intentions and available time.

The elements do not need to compete. Guided rest might open the experience, sound might offer a period of quiet listening, and reflection might help you identify what to take home. Combining modalities is about a coherent experience, not a promise that more techniques produce a larger medical effect.

Think about the return before you leave. A recording, a realistic evening ritual or one clear commitment can make the experience easier to revisit. The value is not only how you feel in the mountains, but whether the choices you make afterward remain kind and workable.

A session that fits your life

For a stay in Aspen or Snowmass, begin with your dates, lodging area and preferred pace. Residents of Basalt and Carbondale may prefer a focused local wellness day. International visitors are welcome to inquire; confirm venue, transport and what is included before making travel arrangements.

A common question

Does a retreat inquiry include accommodation?

Discuss accommodation and transportation separately. Do not assume they are included in a wellness session or custom experience unless they are explicitly listed in your confirmed plan.
