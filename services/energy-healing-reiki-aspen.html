The approach

Less to explain. More room to notice.

Energy work begins with attention: how you are arriving, what feels heavy or scattered, and what you would like from the time. You do not need a particular belief system. You can approach the experience as spiritual practice, gentle body awareness or simply a protected period of rest.

Lauren brings more than ten years as a healing guide and her training as a 10th Generation Usui Reiki Master to this work. A session may include Reiki, polarity balancing, craniosacral-inspired stillness and intuitive energy clearing. These are distinct traditions, and the combination is discussed around your preferences.

Touch is a choice, not an assumption. Light contact and off-body work can be discussed beforehand, and you can change your mind during the session. The aim is a responsive experience rather than a fixed sequence you must endure or complete.

Some people use the language of clearing, activation or reconnection to describe their intentions. Others want to feel less burdened by the pace of daily life. Spiritual themes, including ancestral imagery or past-life exploration, are approached as personal meaning and reflection rather than verified accounts of past events.
