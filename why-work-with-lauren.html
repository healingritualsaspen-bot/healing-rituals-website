The Whole Person.
The Deeper Inner World.

At Healing Rituals, every session is created around the whole person: your intentions, experiences, nervous system, emotional landscape, energy, beliefs, and vision for the life you are ready to create. This is a deeply personalized space where you can feel seen, supported, and completely honored without pressure to perform, explain everything perfectly, or force your way through healing.

For more than ten years, Lauren has guided clients through restorative and transformational experiences that work with the deep subconscious programs that control 95% of your experiences, patterns, emotions, and habits in life. She has found and merged multiple scientifically proven therapies and techniques that have been proven to create the most profound shifts quickly and effortlessly for clients.

She seamlessly weaves sound and vibrational medicine, gentle breath, Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming, Reiki, intuitive energy work, craniosacral and polarity techniques, ceremony, and integrative wellness support. With a background in Health Sciences and advanced training in mind-body and energetic practices, she offers a grounded, deeply intuitive approach.

Over a decade of personalized healing guidance

These Sessions Offer
Another Way Forward.

Clients often arrive carrying more than they realize—stress held in the body, repetitive thoughts, emotional heaviness, self-doubt, relationship patterns, grief, exhaustion, disconnection, or the feeling that they understand what needs to change but cannot seem to create that change through conscious effort alone.

Rather than requiring you to analyze every experience or repeatedly revisit painful details, the work invites you into deep rest and receptive awareness. As the thinking mind becomes quieter, the body can soften, protective patterns can loosen, and new perspectives can become available. Sound, vibration, carefully chosen language, guided imagery, breath, and intuitive energy work are woven together into one continuous healing tapestry designed to support release, reconnection, and meaningful inner change.

Nothing to Perform.
Everything to Receive.

You are welcomed into a private, beautiful, and deeply comfortable environment where the outside world can fall away. Resting on a plush supportive pad, surrounded by immersive sound and vibration, you are invited to move into the dreamlike space between waking and sleep—a state often associated with profound rest, inner imagery, creativity, insight, and subconscious receptivity.

There is nothing you need to achieve during the session. You are not expected to “do healing correctly.” You are simply supported in resting, receiving, feeling, releasing, and allowing the experience to meet you exactly where you are.

The Difference Is
in the Weaving.

What makes working with Lauren different is not simply the number of modalities offered—it is the way they are thoughtfully woven together around each individual. She listens beyond the surface, recognizes the patterns beneath the story, and creates an experience that speaks to the client’s deeper inner world with compassion, sensitivity, and care.

The intention is never to tell you who to become. It is to help quiet the noise, soften what has been held too tightly, and create space for your own wisdom, wholeness, and truth to come forward.

Clients may emerge feeling lighter, clearer, deeply rested, emotionally relieved, more connected to themselves, and able to meet their lives from a different internal foundation. Old stories can begin to lose their power. Self-criticism can soften into compassion. Fear can make room for trust. Confusion can open into clarity. The body may finally feel permission to exhale.

Healing Rituals is for those seeking more than a temporary escape. It is for people ready to experience a profound inward return—a renewed relationship with themselves and a more loving, empowered way of relating to life.
