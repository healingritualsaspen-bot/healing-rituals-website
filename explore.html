A little understanding before you begin

Different ways to pause,
reflect and reconnect.

These guides explain the experiences available through Healing Rituals and the questions worth asking before you choose one. Each topic includes an overview, a practical article and a closer look at relevant research. The collection also connects these approaches with common emotional, relational and energy-related concerns while keeping clinical claims within the evidence.

The practice is based in Aspen and welcomes people from Snowmass, Basalt, Carbondale and across the Roaring Fork Valley, as well as visitors to Colorado. You can explore the reading wherever you live.
