A few helpful details

Private Group
Questions

Can the experience come to our residence, hotel or retreat venue?

Yes. Group journeys can be planned for private residences, retreat venues, hospitality spaces and other suitable settings throughout Aspen, Snowmass, Basalt, Carbondale and the Roaring Fork Valley.

Does everyone need meditation or breathwork experience?

No. The experience is designed to be passive and welcoming. Guests are invited to rest comfortably while sound, guided imagery, gentle breath and vibration carry the journey.

How long is a private group experience?

The format is curated around the group and occasion. Your inquiry can include the available time window, and the final experience will be designed to flow naturally within the gathering.

Can natural plant medicine be part of our group journey?

Yes. Natural plant medicine can be woven into the journey as an intentional element alongside sound, subconscious imagery, gentle breath, energy work and ceremony. Your inquiry helps us understand the group, setting and depth you are envisioning.

Can this be customized around our retreat theme or celebration?

Yes. The language, sound, energetic focus, pacing and ceremonial elements can be shaped around your intention, group dynamic and desired atmosphere.
