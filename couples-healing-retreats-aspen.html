A Different Way to Meet Each Other

Relationship patterns often become difficult to change when both people are reacting from stress, old protection and the need to be understood. A private couples healing retreat in Aspen creates time for each person to soften individually before returning to the relationship with greater presence.

Experiences may begin with separate intention setting and personalized Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming journeys. Sound healing, Reiki, polarity therapy, craniosacral-inspired energy work and gentle breathwork can support deep rest and emotional openness. The couple then comes together for a shared sound, breath or integration experience centered on listening, compassion and the relationship they want to create.

Couples may choose this work alongside ongoing couples therapy or as a restorative relationship wellness experience of its own. Themes can include communication, trust, intimacy, repetitive conflict, forgiveness, shared purpose, major transitions and remembering what each person values in the other.

Common questions

Couples Experiences

Do we complete every part together?

Not necessarily. Private individual work can create space for honesty and personal reflection, followed by shared sound, breath or integration when both partners are ready.

What relationship intentions can we bring?

Couples often focus on communication, trust, intimacy, life transitions, recurring patterns, compassion, appreciation, shared purpose or reconnecting after a difficult season.

Can this complement couples therapy?

Yes. Some couples use a restorative Healing Rituals experience alongside work with their existing licensed couples therapist, bringing greater calm and embodied insight into that process.

Can we receive personalized recordings?

Individual recordings can reinforce each partner’s personal intentions, and a shared integration practice may be created for the relationship.
