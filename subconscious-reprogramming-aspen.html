Change Beneath the Thinking Mind

Some nuance cannot be reached through analysis alone. Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming is Lauren’s signature process for helping the conscious mind become quiet enough for imagery, intentional language, sound and emotional experience to reach deeper patterns.

Before your session, two or three intentions, mantras or areas of desired change become the foundation for a one-of-one guided journey. Every metaphor, affirmation and sensory layer is written around your language and the way you want to feel. Clients rest comfortably while the experience unfolds without performance, striving or pressure to explain.

People seek subconscious reprogramming in Aspen for self-worth, confidence, relationship patterns, emotional freedom, healthier habits, creativity, spiritual connection and a clearer relationship with themselves. The work may be experienced on its own or woven into sound healing, energy work, gentle breathwork and plant-assisted journey support.

Yes. Sound healing, energy work, gentle breathwork, integration and a personalized recording can become part of one continuous healing tapestry.
