A private wellness retreat offers something ordinary appointments cannot: time, beauty, continuity and the freedom to let an experience unfold without rushing back into the outside world. Your experience may weave together Quantum Subconscious Reprogramming, immersive sound healing, Reiki, polarity therapy, craniosacral-inspired energy work, gentle breathwork, intention setting, guided journeying and reflective integration. Every detail begins with purpose. A private half-day, full-day or multi-session experience can be shaped around emotional renewal, nervous-system rest, spiritual exploration, creative clarity, life transition, relationship reconnection or a desire to return home feeling genuinely different.

Common questions

Private Aspen Experiences

Who are private retreats created for?

Experiences are available for individuals, couples and intimate private groups, including visitors traveling from elsewhere in the United States or internationally.

What can be included?

Options include subconscious reprogramming, personalized recordings, sound and vibrational medicine, Reiki, polarity, craniosacral-inspired energy work, breathwork, integration and holistic wellness guidance.

Can an experience be planned around an Aspen vacation?

Yes. The timing and format can be designed as a meaningful part of a stay in Aspen or Snowmass, with enough space before and afterward to receive and integrate.

Do couples work individually or together?

Both are possible. Individual work can honor each person’s private inner landscape, while shared experiences support communication, compassion, emotional resonance and reconnection.
