Before and after a plant-assisted journey: questions worth asking

A beautiful setting is only one part of a supported experience. Preparation includes understanding the people, boundaries and follow-up around it.

From Healing Rituals · 2 min read · Updated September 16, 2026

Clarify the kind of support you are seeking

Are you looking for clinical treatment, spiritual exploration, preparation or help making sense of a past experience? Those are different needs. Ask the provider to describe their role and qualifications in plain language rather than relying on a broad label such as psychedelic therapy.

In Colorado, regulated natural medicine services have a licensing framework. The official DORA resources are a starting point for checking that framework. Do not infer a provider’s license or clinical scope from the appearance of a website.

Ask about screening and choice

Find out who reviews medical and mental-health history, how medication questions are handled and what support is available if distress arises. Do not change prescribed medication without the prescribing clinician’s guidance.

Ask how consent works around touch, privacy and any suggested practices. You should understand what is included and what is optional before an experience begins. Uncertainty is a reason to ask questions, not something you must conceal to be accepted.

Make integration part of the plan

Integration can begin with a plain account of what you remember: images, emotions, questions and moments that felt important. It does not require deciding immediately what everything means.

Choose one manageable way to reflect, and one trusted source of support. A strong feeling can be worth exploring without treating it as an instruction to change a relationship, job or care plan overnight. Ongoing distress calls for qualified support, not pressure to interpret it as progress.

Plan travel around care

If you are coming to Aspen from another state or abroad, clarify suitability, location, fees, transportation and follow-up before booking. Ask who remains available once you return home and how urgent concerns would be handled.

Healing Rituals inquiries can explore intentions, guided-rest options and reflective support. The right next step may be a conversation rather than a booking. You can also choose a sound, energy or subconscious journey without a plant-assisted component.
