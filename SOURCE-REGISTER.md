# Healing Rituals Source Register

Checked: 2026-09-16

## Gollwitzer PM, Sheeran P. Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-analysis of Effects and Processes. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology. 2006;38:69–119.
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1
- Study/source type and limitations: Meta-analysis across varied goal domains; applicability depends on the behavior and context.
- Cited by: How to turn an inner intention into an everyday choice

## Stanhope J, Weinstein P. The human health effects of singing bowls: A systematic review. Complementary Therapies in Medicine. 2020;51:102412.
- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32507429/
- Study/source type and limitations: Systematic review; only four eligible studies and substantial methodological limitations.
- Cited by: When sound feels like too much: choosing a gentler listening experience

## Goldsby TL et al. Effects of Singing Bowl Sound Meditation on Mood, Tension, and Well-being: An Observational Study. J Evid Based Complementary Altern Med. 2017;22(3):401–406.
- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27694559/
- Study/source type and limitations: Single-group observational study; immediate self-reported outcomes, no randomized control.
- Cited by: When sound feels like too much: choosing a gentler listening experience

## National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health. Reiki. Updated 2022.
- Link: https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/reiki
- Study/source type and limitations: Authoritative evidence overview; notes lack of clear evidence for health purposes and no scientific evidence for the proposed energy field.
- Cited by: Consent and comfort in Reiki and intuitive energy work

## National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health. Relaxation Techniques: What You Need To Know.
- Link: https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/relaxation-techniques-what-you-need-to-know
- Study/source type and limitations: Authoritative overview across multiple relaxation techniques; evidence and safety vary by practice and condition.
- Cited by: How to practice deep rest without trying to fall asleep

## Balban MY et al. Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. Cell Reports Medicine. 2023;4(1):100895.
- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/
- Study/source type and limitations: Randomized controlled remote study of 108 adults over 28 days; brief practices and mainly short-term outcomes.
- Cited by: A gentle breath-pacing practice for life’s transitions

## Pilecki B et al. Ethical and legal issues in psychedelic harm reduction and integration therapy. Harm Reduction Journal. 2021;18:40.
- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33827588/
- Study/source type and limitations: Ethics/legal analysis and practice model, not an outcome trial of integration.
- Cited by: Psychedelic integration when the meaning is not immediately clear

## Cohen M et al. The health impact of residential retreats: a systematic review. BMC Complementary Medicine and Therapies. 2017;17:526.
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-017-2078-4
- Study/source type and limitations: Review of heterogeneous retreat programs; selection bias, varied interventions and limited long-term follow-up constrain conclusions.
- Cited by: How to bring a wellness retreat home without recreating the retreat

## Karremans JC et al. Comparing the effects of a mindfulness versus relaxation intervention on romantic relationship wellbeing. Scientific Reports. 2020;10:21696.
- Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-78919-6
- Study/source type and limitations: Randomized brief-intervention study focused on immediate conflict-related outcomes; not a couples-therapy trial.
- Cited by: Listening without fixing: a quieter practice for couples
